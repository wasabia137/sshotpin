📸 Region capture

DragSelect the area to capture
Drag a blue dotResize the selection
Drag insideMove the selection
Tool buttons✏️Pen ↗Arrow ▭Rect ◯Ellipse 🖍Highlighter 🔲Mosaic ①Numbers
↩ / Ctrl+Z / right-clickUndo drawing
Enter or 📌Pin on screen
Ctrl+C / Ctrl+SCopy / save as PNG
🙈 buttonCover the selected area
EscDeselect tool; press again to cancel
Great for shooting material and adding an arrow before showing it. Pick a tool, draw inside the area, then press Enter.

📌 Pins — images stuck on screen

DragMove
Drag a cornerResize (keeps ratio)
WheelZoom in / out
Ctrl+wheelOpacity
Double-clickCollapse / expand
✕ button · wheel click · EscClose (✕ appears top-right on hover)
0Original size & opacity
1 / 2Rotate 90° cw / ccw
3 / 4Flip horizontally / vertically
Right-clickBorder color, zoom & opacity, click-through
With click-through on, you can use the programs underneath while the pin stays on top. Turn it off via tray → "Turn off all click-through".
